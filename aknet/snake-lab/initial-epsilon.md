# Initial Epsilon

Initial epsilon is the probability of choosing a random action at the beginning of training.

- Higher values begin with more exploration.
- Lower values begin with more use of the current Q-function.
- Akbar proposes a baseline and the experiment runs values five percent below and above it as well as the baseline.
- Initial epsilon must be interpreted together with epsilon decay.
