# Snake Game

In the Snake game, the snake starts in the center of the board. A piece of food is placed in a random unoccupied square. The AI can continue straight, turn left, or turn right on each move.

The objective is to achieve a high score. The snake gets one point and grows one segment each time it eats food. A new piece of food is then placed in a random unoccupied square.

The game ends when the snake hits itself or a wall. It also ends when the move limit is exceeded, preventing an episode from continuing indefinitely without progress.

The board is 20 by 20 squares. Food occupies one square, and the snake initially occupies three squares.
